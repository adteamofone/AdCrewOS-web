# AdCrewOS 👋

**Your ads, on autopilot.**

AdCrewOS watches your Google and Meta (Facebook & Instagram) ads for you — all day,
every day. If an ad starts wasting money, it hits the brakes for you. If an ad is doing
great, it asks you (with one tap) if you want to give it more budget. You get your time
back, and your money stays safe.

This guide is for **everyone** — you do not need to be techy to use AdCrewOS. If you can
use email, you can use this. 🙂

---

## What AdCrewOS does for you

- 🛡️ **Protects your money.** If an ad spends too much or stops working well, AdCrewOS
  **pauses it automatically** so you don't burn cash while you're asleep or busy.
- 📈 **Grows your winners.** When an ad is doing really well, AdCrewOS sends you a message
  and you just tap **Approve** to give it more budget. Nothing big happens without your OK.
- 🔔 **Tells you what's going on.** You get an email or a text message the moment something
  important happens. No need to keep checking.
- 🧑‍💼 **Looks professional.** Agencies can add their own logo to the reports they send clients.

## Getting started (about 5 minutes)

### Step 1 — Make your account
1. Go to the AdCrewOS website and click **"Start free trial."**
2. Type your name, your email, and pick a password.
3. Choose a plan:
   - **Solo** — perfect if you run your own ads. (1 Google account + 1 Meta account.)
   - **Agency** — for people who run ads for lots of clients. (Up to 10 of each.)
4. You get **7 days free**. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel
   anytime.

### Step 2 — Set up your safety net (the wizard)
After you sign up, a short setup wizard walks you through 4 easy screens:
1. **Alerts** — type your phone number if you want text messages (optional).
2. **Connect your ads** — link your Google and/or Meta account with one click.
   - 👉 **Not ready yet? Click "Skip → use demo data."** This fills the app with
     pretend numbers so you can look around and see how everything works, with zero risk.
3. **Goals** — tell it what a good result looks like (for example, "I don't want to pay
   more than $40 to get a sale").
4. **Guardrails** — pick the point where AdCrewOS should **pause** an ad to protect you,
   and the point where it should **ask you** about spending more. Not sure? The suggested
   numbers are a great starting point.

Click **"Enter cockpit"** and you're done. 🎉

### Step 3 — Use your cockpit (the main screen)
The **Cockpit** is your home base. Here's what you'll see:
- **The four boxes up top** — a quick snapshot: how many ad accounts you have, how much
  you've spent today, how many ads got auto-paused, and how many "grow this?" suggestions
  are waiting for you.
- **The account cards** — one card per ad account, each with a simple line chart so you can
  see how things are trending.
- **The Alert feed** — a running list of what AdCrewOS did and what it wants to ask you.
  When a "grow this ad?" suggestion appears, just tap **Approve** or **Dismiss**. That's it.

## The everyday routine

Honestly? There isn't much of one — that's the whole point. 😄

- Once a day (or whenever you get an alert), open the Cockpit.
- Read the Alert feed.
- Tap **Approve** on any good ideas, **Dismiss** the rest.
- Get on with your day.

AdCrewOS handles the constant watching and the emergency "pause it now!" moments for you,
around the clock.

## Reports (great for agencies)

Go to **Reports** to create a clean, good-looking PDF of how the ads are doing. You can
send it to yourself or to a client. On the **Agency** plan, your own logo goes right on top.

## Settings — where to change things

Click **Settings** to:
- **Billing** — change your plan, update your card, or cancel (opens a secure Stripe page).
- **Alerts** — choose email, text, or both.
- **Connected accounts** — add or remove Google/Meta accounts.
- **Branding** — upload your agency logo for reports (Agency plan).

## Common questions

**Will it spend my money without asking?**
No. AdCrewOS only ever does one thing on its own: **pause** an ad to protect you.
Spending *more* always waits for your tap.

**What if I don't connect a real ad account yet?**
Use **demo data** during setup. You'll see exactly how it works with pretend numbers,
totally risk-free.

**Can I cancel?**
Yes, anytime, from **Settings → Billing**. No phone calls, no hoops.

**Is my login safe?**
Yes. Your ad-account connections are stored encrypted, and payments are handled by Stripe,
the same company trusted by huge brands worldwide.
